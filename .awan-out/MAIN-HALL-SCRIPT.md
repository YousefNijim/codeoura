# Main Hall Team — Briefing
**Voice-over script (English) · Pullman Istanbul · Awan Al-Sharq 2026**

Record each block as its own file, named `hall_1` … `hall_8` **in this order**.
I transcribe every file before mixing, so a mix-up gets caught — but matching
names saves a round. Targets are a guide; the video is retimed to whatever you
actually record.

---

### 1 — OPEN  *(~14s)*
The main hall. One thousand six hundred and eighty square metres.
Two thousand seats. Ten of you.
This is the hardest room at the conference, and the most visible.
Here is how we hold it.

### 2 — THE HALL  *(~18s)*
One stage. Three reserved VIP rows at the front. Two blocks of seating with a
centre aisle. And four doors, all on the same wall.
Door one sits closest to the stage. Door four is at the back.
Learn these numbers. Every instruction today uses them.

### 3 — VIP · 2 PEOPLE  *(~24s)*
Two of you are on VIP, and only VIP.
One stands at Door one — welcome them, check them, point them in.
Door one is the VIP entrance. Do not let it become a general door.
The second of you is inside at the front, seating them in rows one to three.
Those rows are reserved. Nobody else sits there, however empty the room looks.

### 4 — DOORS 2, 3, 4 · 3 PEOPLE  *(~25s)*
Three of you take doors two, three and four. One usher per door.
Your job is flow. Keep people moving in, keep the doorway clear, and stop a
queue before it forms.
If a crowd builds at your door, call your lead. Do not try to hold it alone.
And do not leave your door to seat someone. Your door is your post.

### 5 — SEATING & MICROPHONE · 5 PEOPLE  *(~26s)*
The remaining five work inside the hall.
Before the session, guide people to their seats, and fill from the front.
An empty front row on camera is a problem.
During open sessions you are also the microphone. Bring it to the speaker, stay
with them, take it back, and carry it to the next person.
Never hand it off and walk away.

### 6 — THE SWITCH  *(~28s)*
The moment the session starts, everything changes.
Doors one, two and three close. Door four, the last door, stays open.
That is the only way in or out while a session is running.
Someone at a closed door gets sent to Door four. Politely, every time.
One exception: core team, and anyone wearing the white Full Access sticker, go
through any door at any time. Do not stop them.

### 7 — TWO SECTIONS, TWO LEADS  *(~21s)*
The hall is too big for one person to run, so we split it.
Laybah takes section one. Aya takes section two.
Know your section, and know your lead. Go to them with anything.
If your lead is not in the hall, go to the other one. Never sit on a problem
waiting for one person to come back.

### 8 — CLOSE  *(~14s)*
Four doors. Three VIP rows. Two sections. Ten of you.
Fill the front. Hold the doors. Move the mic. Keep the room quiet.
And if you are not sure — ask your lead. Never guess.
