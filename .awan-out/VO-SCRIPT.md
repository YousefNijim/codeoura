### 00:00 — TITLE  *(target 9s)*
Awan Al-Sharq twenty twenty-six. Pullman Istanbul. Two days, one venue,
thirty-six ushers. This is your floor.

### 00:10 — THE VENUE  *(target 19s)*
Everything happens across two levels. Ground floor: the main entrance,
registration, the exhibition, and the main hall. Upstairs: the meeting rooms.
Guests move through this building in one direction, and five teams carry them
along it. This is that chain.

### 00:30 — 1 · TRANSITION  *(target 23s)*
It starts at the hotel doors. Six people. The Transition team.
You are the first face of this conference. Welcome every guest before they step
inside. Ask if they have the conference app, help them find their QR code, and
send them to the right desk. Protocol, speakers and VIPs go to VIP registration,
and you coordinate that directly. Keep the doorway moving. Nobody stops at the
entrance.

### 00:54 — 2 · REGISTRATION  *(target 21s)*
Just past the doors: the queue lines and the registration desks. Six people.
You scan the QR, you complete anyone who is not registered yet, and you hand
over the badge. Thirteen badge categories, and each one decides where that guest
is allowed to go. One queue, kept straight, kept fast. The entrance behind you
depends on it.

### 01:16 — 3 · ACTIVATION  *(target 21s)*
Between registration and the main hall sits Activation. Eight people.
This is the access line. No badge, no entry. That is the whole rule.
You also cover the side doors from the hotel, because those are the ways in that
nobody registered through. Check calmly, check everyone, never argue. If someone
insists, you call your lead. You do not handle it alone.

### 01:38 — 4 · MAIN HALL  *(target 27s)*
Inside the hall: ten people. The biggest team, and the hardest one.
Fill the front rows first. An empty front row on camera is a problem.
Guide guests to their seats, respect reserved seating, keep the aisles and the
doors clear, and keep the room quiet once a session starts.
At a break, you are the one who moves the room out, on time, in coordination
with Breakouts. And remember: hall preparation slots are not breaks. People stay
inside.

### 02:06 — 5 · BREAKOUTS  *(target 23s)*
When the hall empties, Breakouts takes over. Six people, out on the exhibition
floor. Coffee breaks and lunch. Your job is to spread people out, across the
booths, the sitting areas, the food area, the photo corners, and never let one
point jam. Watch the clock. Two minutes before a session resumes you start
moving people back, and you hand them to Transition.

### 02:30 — UPSTAIRS  *(target 19s)*
After the afternoon sessions, guests move up for the parallel meetings.
Yaqoot one to five. Turquoise one to five. The Sapphire Room is yours, the
volunteer room. Transition owns the stairs and the elevators. Let people exit
before others enter, do not let the elevator area crowd, and if you are not sure
which room, do not guess. Confirm it.

### 02:50 — CLOSE  *(target 21s)*
Five teams. One guest, one path. Door, desk, badge, seat, break, and back again.
Every handover is another team trusting you to deliver that guest on time.
Know your area. Know your lead. Stay visible, stay calm, never guess.
One team. One experience. One smooth conference.
